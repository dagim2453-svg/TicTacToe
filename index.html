• The game is played on a 3×3 grid
  • players take turns, one using "X" and the other "O"
  • Players alternate placing their symbol in any empty square.
  • Align three of your symbols horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
  • The first player to complete a line of three symbols wins.
  • If all squares are filled without a winning line, the game ends in a tie.
